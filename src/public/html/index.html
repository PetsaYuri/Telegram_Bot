Welcome to Classroom Helper

Manage your classroom efficiently with our tool. Classroom Helper is a Telegram bot that helps users view and manages their classroom workspaces.

How It Works

  1. Start the bot by searching for @classroom_helper_bot on Telegram.
  2. Enter /menu to begin interacting with the bot.
  3. If authentication is required, click the link the bot provides to sign in with Google.
  4. After signing in, you will be redirected back to Telegram, and the bot will continue assisting you.

Authentication & Permissions

  • This bot uses Google authentication to access your Google Classroom data securely. We only request the necessary permissions to provide you with the best experience.
