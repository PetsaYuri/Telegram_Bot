Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11.03.2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Classroom Helper collects, processes, and protects your information when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following data when you use the application:

  • Authentication Data: OAuth2 authentication tokens to access Google Classroom.
  • Course Data: Course ID, course title, and last retrieval timestamp (only if accessed through our service).
  • Encrypted Refresh Token: Stored securely but saved in encrypted format within the database.
  • Telegram Chat ID: If connected via Telegram, we store the chat ID to enable bot interactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use this data to:

  • Authenticate and retrieve Google Classroom data on your behalf.
  • Improve and personalize user experience.
  • Enable Telegram-based interactions if opted in.
  • Ensure security and prevent unauthorized access.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • We retain user data only as long as necessary to provide our services.
  • If you revoke Google access, all associated data (including classroom data and Telegram chat ID) is deleted within 30 days.
  • You can request immediate data deletion by contacting us at: petsa.yurii@student.uzhnu.edu.ua.
  • Refresh tokens are stored in an encrypted format but as plain text in the database, meaning they are secured but still exist in a retrievable state.

5. Data Sharing & Security

  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • OAuth2 authentication tokens are encrypted before storage, but they remain stored in the database in an accessible format.
  • We follow industry security standards to protect sensitive information.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke Google access at any time.
  • Request deletion of stored data.
  • Contact us for any data privacy concerns.

7. Contact Us

For any questions or privacy concerns, please contact us at: petsa.yurii@student.uzhnu.edu.ua
